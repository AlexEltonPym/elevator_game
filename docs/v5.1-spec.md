## 1. Atrium orientation (R-3)
The transfer atrium is currently 2 tall; make it **2 wide × 1 tall**. Its two dropoffs become
a LEFT dock (one lift) and a RIGHT dock (the other), matching a horizontal 2-wide room. Keep the
A→cross→C transfer working.

## 2. Real walk time (deterministic, priced, animated)
Today the in-room walk (spawn tile → dock; alight dock → destination/next-dock) is an instant hop
with only a flat pathfinding constant. Make it cost REAL game-time:
- `WALK_PER_TILE` seconds per tile of Manhattan distance walked (pick a value that reads well,
  ~0.3–0.6 s; tune for feel). Board = walk from the passenger's tile to the dock; alight = walk
  from the dock to the destination tile (or the next dock on a transfer).
- It must actually delay the passenger in the SIM: while walking they are not yet boardable / not
  yet counted served; the elevator does not wait on them beyond its normal door cycle (a passenger
  still walking when the doors close catches the next stop — same rule as v4 late arrivals).
- Keep it SIMPLE and deterministic so the optimizer can still price it exactly: no collision, no
  congestion, no queuing interaction — just `dist * WALK_PER_TILE`. `Pathfind5` leg cost uses the
  SAME formula (replace the flat WALK constant with the distance-based one) so planning stays
  honest against the sim.
- Determinism: fixed-step, reproducible; a repeated run must be identical.

### Animation (simple, programmer-art)
Make the walk legible (fixes the R-3 "reads as teleporting?" note): the passenger figure TWEENS
its position from tile to tile over the walk duration, and slides ON to the car when boarding /
OFF the car to the dock when alighting. A moving shape, not sprites. Riders should visibly enter
and leave rather than pop. Purely visual — must not affect the deterministic sim/timing.

## 3. One-lift corridors (width-2 default mutex)
Port the corridor-width mechanic from v4 (it lived in grid3/car3/main3 as gate groups with a
width) into v5, under the SAME width model already in v5 (1 = express/tiny, 2 = normal, 3 = cargo):
- Corridor cells carry a width; a car may enter iff `car.width <= corridor.width`; cars share a
  corridor while the sum of widths inside it `<= corridor.width`. Contiguous corridor cells form
  one mutex group (FIFO), exactly as v4.
- **Default corridor width 2** = one NORMAL elevator at a time (a normal car is width 2, and
  2+2 > 2), i.e. a one-lift corridor — the "snakes can't share a tile" constraint. A width-1
  corridor is express/pod-only; a width-3 corridor fits a cargo or several small.
- Draw corridors with the v4 hazard-stripe identity (calm, per the UI pass) + the occupied tint.
- Add corridors to the prototype: put a one-lift (width-2) corridor into R-4 (or a new R-5
  "Bottleneck") so the constraint is felt — two lifts wanting the same corridor must queue or one
  must detour. Keep it a clear, single teaching instance, not everywhere.

NOTE (do NOT build): a "speedup / bonus block" a route can pass through is a future idea only.

**"serves 0"** for served routes. Fix the count to reflect the rooms the committed route actually
reaches (via their dock cells). Verify it updates as the route is drawn/redrawn.
