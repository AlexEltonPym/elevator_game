# v3.4 — loop routes

If a drawn path closes back onto its own head, the car LOOPS (always travels forward
around the cycle) instead of ping-ponging. Loops are ONE-WAY: a loop serves A→B in the
travel direction quickly and B→A only the long way around — that asymmetry is the
strategic point, and pathfinding must model it honestly.

## Drawing / closing

- While drawing, dragging onto the stroke's HEAD cell (stroke[0]) when the current last
  cell is orthogonally adjacent to it and the stroke has ≥ 4 cells CLOSES the loop. This
  is the one exception to the collision rule (any other own-stroke touch still collides;
  backtrack-the-way-you-came still retracts).
- A closed live stroke: draw the closing segment in the preview and stop extending
  (further forward drags are ignored). Reversing back onto the tail cell REOPENS the loop
  (removes the closing link) and normal backtrack continues from there.
- Release commits as usual. Route3 gains `closed: bool`. A closed route still needs ≥ 2
  room stops to run.
- Visual: committed loops render the closing segment like any other; add small direction
  arrows (chevrons along the line every few cells) on CLOSED routes only, showing travel
  direction = the order the player drew in. Ping-pong routes stay arrow-free.

## Movement

- Closed route: the car advances idx → (idx+1) mod n forever; no reversing, no end dwell.
  Ping-pong behavior unchanged for open routes.
- Gates, door cycles, boarding, capacity: unchanged. Recall (redeploy) on a closed route
  drives FORWARD to the nearest room stop (never backwards).
- Idle rule unchanged (car parks when no one aboard/planning to board it); `home_cell`
  deadhead on a loop also travels forward.

## Pathfinding (the important part)

- Route3.ride_dist becomes direction-aware: on a closed route the distance from stop a to
  stop b is ((idx_b - idx_a) mod n) cells — forward-only. Open routes keep |a-b|.
- Passenger cost/legs use that directional distance, so a "backward" trip on a loop is
  honestly priced as almost-a-full-lap and passengers will prefer another route or a
  transfer when one is cheaper. No other pathfind changes.

## Level: L4 "Ring" (new, before X-1 in the list)

> **PARTLY SUPERSEDED, v3.5 level pass.** The one-cell ring below shipped and then
> measured badly: 45 % of *uniformly random* route-sets won it outright, and the
> optimizer beat the "three staggered clockwise loops" thesis by +24 with leaner
> partial arcs. On a one-cell ring with adjacent-group demand, almost any decodable
> plan is a decent plan — the level had no floor. L4 is now a **two-lane** ring (outer
> lane past every room, inner lane past none) with **cross-building** demand (every
> trip is half a lap), so the answer is to WEAVE a closed loop in and out: swing to the
> outer lane only for your own crowd. The loop mechanic this spec exists for is
> untouched and is still what the level teaches; what changed is that hugging the outer
> lane now costs eight door cycles a lap, so "close it and cover everything" is the
> honest-beginner trap rather than the answer. Details: `scripts/v3/levels3.gd`,
> `docs/depth-report.md`.


A ring-shaped building (donut: blocked core, one-cell-wide ring corridor of rooms and
open cells, a couple of gate cells optional if it helps the thesis). Demand is strongly
DIRECTIONAL (e.g. a morning-commute cycle: homes → offices → canteen → homes, each hop
clockwise). Thesis: a closed loop drawn clockwise one-ways the crowd (+ support cards as
needed). Naive: the same coverage as ping-pong lines (honest: what a player who hasn't
discovered closing draws — e.g. one long ping-pong C-shape + supports). Naive must LOSE
(cars waste half their time driving back empty against the flow), thesis WINS with
lost ≤ 3. Cards: builder's choice within existing types.

Design note: with a one-cell ring, all demand reachable by one loop — make quota/demand
high enough that ping-pong's direction-reversal (double the effective headway on the far
side) is fatal but the loop cruises. Counter-clockwise trickle demand (small %) is good
seasoning: the loop prices it as the long way and still absorbs it.

## Harness

- New mechanic checks: closing detection (≥4 cells + adjacency; a 3-cell stroke cannot
  close), reopen-by-backtrack, closed car wraps without reversing (sample idx over time),
  directional ride_dist (a→b short, b→a = n - short), a passenger with backward demand
  routes the long way or transfers, recall-on-loop drives forward.
- L4 scenario pair (naive/thesis as data in scripts/v3/scenarios3.gd), assertions:
  L4 naive LOSES, L4 thesis WINS lost ≤ 3. Global lints (no dead rooms, thesis covers all
  rooms) apply to L4 automatically. Existing assertions all stay green.
- Watch mode gets L4 rows for free (PLAY / WATCH NAIVE / WATCH THESIS).
