# v3.3 — redeploy mechanic, L2 gate-using thesis, L3 without dead rooms

Three changes + harness upgrades. (The drag-back collision fix shipped separately:
retract only by reversing the way you came; any other touch of your own stroke is a
collision.)

## 1. Redeploy mechanic (no more teleporting cars)

Committing a new route (or CLEAR) mid-game no longer teleports the car. Sequence:

1. **Recall**: if the car has riders, it keeps running its OLD route just long enough to
   reach the nearest room stop (by ride distance), doors cycle, ALL riders step out and
   replan. An empty car skips straight to step 2 from wherever it is.
2. **Redeploy**: the car vanishes from the grid (quick shrink/fade at its cell) and a
   ghost outline + countdown appears at the NEW route's start cell. After
   `REDEPLOY_DELAY = 3.0` s (game-time, so pause holds it) the car appears there and
   starts service. CLEAR ends at step 1: after recall the car simply vanishes and its
   chip shows unrouted.
3. Re-commit while recalling/redeploying just retargets the pending route; the countdown
   restarts only if the start cell changed.

Session-start commits (harness scenarios, watch-mode pre-draw, and the first commit of
each card in normal play when the car was never deployed) deploy INSTANTLY — the delay is
the cost of MID-GAME redesign, not of designing.

Pathfinding note: passengers already replan on commit and plan against routes, not car
positions; the 7 s per-leg wait constant absorbs the redeploy gap. No pathfind changes.

## 2. L2 — the thesis must USE the gate

Keep the corridor and the two-cluster shape, but the winning strategy must send traffic
THROUGH the gate, not around it entirely. Redesign demand (and the grid if needed) so:

- The thesis route set includes at least one route through the gate corridor, and that
  route does real work (harness: thesis run has ≥ 10 gate transits).
- The perimeter still earns its place (e.g. execs bottom↔top ride a perimeter express
  while mid-cluster↔mid-cluster demand crosses via the gate — middle rooms are far from
  the perimeter by construction, so the gate is their honest best path).
- Naive (all cards full height through the gate) still LOSES; thesis WINS with lost ≤ 3.

The lesson becomes: "the gate is a resource to spend wisely, not a trap to avoid."

## 3. L3 — no dead rooms

Zero-demand decoy rooms are out — every room in every level must generate and receive
demand (small weights are fine; zero is not). Redesign L3 so the naive-vs-thesis contrast
comes from real geometry: direct winding routes lose because they're LONG and stop-heavy
while covering all rooms, feeders+hub+express win while ALSO serving every room. Remove
the decoys (convert to blocked/corridor or give them genuine demand and coverage in the
thesis routes). Thesis must serve every room (every room reachable in its network), win
with lost ≤ 3, transfer share ≥ 40% on arm→penthouse; naive still LOSES.

## Harness upgrades

- New global lint across ALL levels: every `R` cell has spawn weight > 0 AND is a possible
  destination, and every thesis route set covers every room (each room on some route).
  X-1's thesis/smoke set must comply too (extend it if needed) — no special cases.
- New assertion: L2 thesis gate transits ≥ 10.
- New smoke check for redeploy: mid-run, replace a route that has riders aboard; assert
  riders are dropped at an old-route room (not teleported), the car is absent from the
  grid during the countdown, service resumes from the new start after ~3 s, and a
  session-start commit deploys with no delay.
- All existing assertions stay: L1/L2/L3 naive LOSES, thesis WINS lost ≤ 3, X-1 smoke,
  L3 transfer share. Tune demand numbers, not assertions. Thesis spot-check on ≥ 2
  alternate seeds stays comfortable.
